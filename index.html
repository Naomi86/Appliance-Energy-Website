Understanding Energy Consumption

Household appliances use electricity to perform everyday tasks. Understanding their energy use can help Australian households make informed decisions about appliance use and energy efficiency.

Energy Usage

Different appliances consume different amounts of electricity depending on their power rating and operating time.

Smart Choices

Choosing efficient appliances and reducing unnecessary usage can help manage household electricity consumption.

Australian Homes

Energy consumption varies between households depending on appliance types, usage habits, and home size.

Display Technology
